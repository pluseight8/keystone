# Safety and Privacy

Safety analysis is local-first. Personal/local domains are not destructively moderated by the shared-content policy. If a personal object is shared, only the outgoing projection is evaluated; the personal copy remains intact when distribution is refused.

External AI is not a safety backend for private content. Optional review receives only bounded content and safety context. Any compatible local runtime (built-in, llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio or a custom localhost adapter) is allowed; the model is untrusted compute and cannot change policy or execute tools.

Behavior protection is recipient-local. Blocks reject applicable DMs, invites, visits, tasks, presence/location, discovery and notifications. Mute suppresses surfaces without pretending to revoke identity. Unknown peers receive conservative local quotas. Keystone does not infer a real-world person or deanonymize identities.
